Who is collecting trading cards?

1 · Overview

How big is each card world?

Pokémon draws far more searches than any rival. The words each game repeats show what it is built around.

2 · Themes & structure

What is a collection made of?

Collectors chase special cards. In Pokémon, almost every era adds a new special type, and their share keeps rising.

3 · Collecting trends

How has collecting changed?

Rare tiers now fill more of every year’s cards, and Pokémon and Yu-Gi-Oh release sets on very different rhythms.

4 · Around the world

Where in the world?

Pokémon takes over half of card-game searches in every country. The runner-up changes from place to place.

5 · Conclusion

A boom at the top, a changing of the guard below

Pokémon searches roughly quadrupled after 2024, while One Piece overtook Yu-Gi-Oh for second place in late 2025.

About this visualisation

Who it is for. Malaysians curious about trading card games, from casual buyers to collectors; no expertise needed.

How to read it. Less familiar charts have a “How to read” line. Hover for exact values. On maps, use Zoom and Map Centre.

Things to know. Google Trends values are relative (100 = each query’s peak week), not search counts. Pokémon card names are counted by species; other games by repeated words.

Colour. Each game keeps one colour on every chart (key at the top). The first chart highlights Pokémon only; other games are grey.
